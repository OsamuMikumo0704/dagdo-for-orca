# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect regardless of background, identity, language or
experience. Give actionable feedback, welcome questions and credit others' work.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing private information
without consent are not acceptable.

Report concerns through a private repository security report when they involve
private information, or flag abusive content using GitHub's reporting tools.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect the
community. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of those involved.
